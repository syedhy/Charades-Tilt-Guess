# Charades Tilt Guess - Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 2026

Charades Tilt Guess is built with privacy and offline capabilities at its core.

## Data Collection
**Charades Tilt Guess does not collect any personal data.** 
There are no third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, crash reporters, or advertising networks included in this app.

## Offline and Local Storage
The core game (including custom decks, default decks, and Picture Mode) operates entirely offline. 
Any custom decks you create are stored locally on your device using `FileManager` and your settings are saved locally using `UserDefaults`. None of this data ever leaves your device.

## Internet Usage
The app only connects to the internet in one scenario:
1. **Buy Me a Coffee (Tips):** If you choose to support the developer, the app connects to Apple's StoreKit servers to process the in-app purchase securely. We do not process or store any payment information.

## App Store Privacy Label
Because we do not collect data, the appropriate App Store privacy label is **"Data Not Collected"**.
